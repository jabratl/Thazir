Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Thazir ("the app") is a free web browser for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, and what happens when you choose to sign in with Google for cloud backup.

Data stored on your device

Thazir keeps your browsing data on your own device: bookmarks, home tiles, history, settings, recent tabs, downloads, saved passwords and the Vault. Saved passwords and Vault content are encrypted on the device. We do not have a server that receives this data, and we cannot see it.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google, the app asks for these permissions:

  • Google Drive app data folder (drive.appdata): a private folder that only Thazir can see. Thazir uses it to save and restore your backup. The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Email address and basic profile (userinfo.email, userinfo.profile): used only to show which account is signed in inside the app.

The backup contains only the parts you switch on (bookmarks, home tiles, history, settings, recent tabs). It does not include the Vault or your saved passwords. The backup is sent directly from your device to your own Google Drive. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

How Google data is handled

  • Your Google data is used only to provide the backup and restore feature you asked for.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your Google data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • No person reads your Google data. It stays between your device and Google.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Websites you visit

When you browse, your device connects directly to the websites you open, and those sites have their own privacy practices. Thazir includes content blocking features, and it does not add its own analytics or advertising trackers.

Other network requests

If you open Settings > About > Version history, the app may fetch the public release list from GitHub. This request does not include your browsing data.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Remove Thazir's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or use Sign out inside the app.
  • Your backup lives in Thazir's private app data folder in your Drive. Removing the app's access from your Google account also lets you delete that data.
  • Local data can be deleted from the app's delete browsing data options or by uninstalling the app.

Children

Thazir is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children under 13.
