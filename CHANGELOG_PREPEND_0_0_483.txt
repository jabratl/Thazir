0.0.483 (NEW: Incognito tabs stay until you close them all; the native downloader works in Incognito tabs)
- Base = 0.0.482. Changed files: PrivateTabs.kt, TabManager.kt, SettingsScreen.kt, SiteWindow.kt, DownloadsApi.kt, DownloadService.kt, app/build.gradle.kts. No new permission, no new dependency.
- 1. Incognito tabs are kept: close the app (or it is killed) and the Incognito tabs are still in the tab switcher when you come back, with their logins. They are only gone when you close all of them (Close all Incognito tabs, the Incognito notification, or closing the last one by hand). A normal tab is still the one you see at start; tap an Incognito tab to load it. Settings > Privacy and security > Incognito > "Keep Incognito tabs" (ON by default; OFF = the old behaviour, everything is wiped when the app closes).
- 2. Downloads from an Incognito tab now use the Incognito cookies / login (before, the normal cookies were used, so a file behind the Incognito login failed). Applies to links, images and blob files of an Incognito tab, the Ask-before-download sheet and the Media Grabber. An Incognito download never touches the normal cookies.
- NOT kept: Incognito tab groups, and the Back / Forward list of an Incognito tab (only the address).
- NOT tested: Gradle build (CI), device. Written without a Kotlin compiler. If CI shows an error look at the lines marked "// 0.0.483": TabManager.kt (restorePrivate, saveSession, restoreSession, close), DownloadService.kt (cookieManagerOf, WebJar, privClient, open), DownloadsApi.kt (fromPrivate, isPrivWeb).
