0.0.317 (FIX: crash "ForegroundServiceDidNotStartInTimeException" in WebAudioService)
- Root cause: Background play starts WebAudioService with startForegroundService(), but the service only called startForeground() inside onStartCommand, after other work, and not at all when it was stopped, failed or restarted first. Coming back to the app quickly (onResume -> stopService) could also overtake the start. Android then kills Thazir after about 5 s.
- Fix: WebAudioService calls startForeground() first thing in onCreate and again at the start of every onStartCommand (also for the Stop action); if it cannot, it stops itself. MediaPrefs.onResume stops the service 0.8 s later (cancelled by a new onPause start) so a stop never overtakes a start.
- Files: WebAudioService.kt, MediaPrefs.kt, build.gradle.kts, CHANGELOG, HANDOFF.
- Could break: the Background play notification can show for under a second when you come back to the app quickly. If Android refuses startForeground (no permission / not allowed), the service just ends and play stops in the background.
- NOT tested: Gradle build (CI), device.

- chrome.runtime.getContexts returns the hidden document, the engine (BACKGROUND) and extension tabs, with contextTypes / documentUrls / tabIds filters.
- Menu > Engine status shows the hidden document state.

MGB 0.0.9 (S8)
- Stream detection: pages are watched while they load. HLS/DASH playlists, subtitles, video/audio files and stream-looking requests are reported to the engine (V77 webRequest), including the Referer the page used, so V77 can list them and download with the right headers.
- Header rules: declarativeNetRequest (V77 Referer/Origin/site-header rules) now works for the engine and for extension pages (downloader, player, manga).
- Extension pages opened as tabs now load remote files through the engine proxy, so cross-site requests are no longer blocked.
- Direct downloads send the Referer the page used for that host.
- Menu > Engine status shows header rule counts and sniffer counters.
