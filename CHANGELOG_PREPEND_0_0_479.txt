0.0.479 (FIX: Share / link from another app opened a second Thazir window; ads can no longer open Chrome / other apps by themselves)
- Base = 0.0.478. Changed files: MainActivity.kt, AppNag.kt, TabManager.kt, app/build.gradle.kts. No new permission, no new dependency, manifest unchanged.
- 1. SECOND WINDOW: sharing a link to Thazir from another app (Brave share sheet, other apps) opened a second Thazir card with its own tabs and without the Incognito tab, because MainActivity is launchMode standard and onNewIntent never ran. Now a Share / link while a main window is alive goes to that window (onNewIntent), the window comes to the front, the extra instance closes at once. Site windows (own card per site) and a normal start are not touched. The manifest stays as it is (singleTask would break the site windows).
- 2. ADS OPENING CHROME: an ad page could start Chrome (intent://, googlechrome://) with no tap, again and again when Thazir came back to the front. Now any app / non-web address that comes without a tap, or from a pop-up tab, is refused (toast \"App open blocked, staying in the browser\", at most once per 2.5 s); a pop-up tab that tries it is closed. Even after a tap, a link that only pushes you into another browser (Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Opera, Edge ...) is refused. Normal app links after a tap (maps, YouTube, tel) still open. Uses the existing switch (Settings > app open block, default ON).
- 3. POP-UPS: a tab you are not looking at (not the active tab) can no longer open a window.
- COULD BREAK: (1) a site that opens an app by script without a tap (login by app, payment app) is refused, as in Chrome: tap the button again or turn the switch OFF; (2) a site that really wants Open in Chrome after a tap is refused; (3) mailto / tel started by a script without a tap are refused.
- NOT tested: Gradle build (CI), device. Written without a Kotlin compiler. If CI shows an error: MainActivity.kt (onCreate start, companion mainRef), AppNag.kt (blocksExternal), TabManager.kt (shouldOverrideUrlLoading, onCreateWindow).
